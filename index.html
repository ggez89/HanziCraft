About HanziCraft

Self-Contained Chinese Writing Worksheets

HanziCraft creates printable A4 worksheets for practicing Chinese character handwriting. Each sheet follows the traditional Tianzige (田字格) and Mizige (米字格) balance grid conventions used in Chinese calligraphy education.

100% Offline & Self-Contained

Pīnyīn transliteration runs locally via pinyin-pro. Stroke order vectors are bundled locally via hanzi-writer-data. Embedded fonts include clean sans-serif WenQuanYi Zen Hei and Noto Sans. No external APIs or internet access required.

Worksheets are generated locally in vector PDF format, optimized for physical A4 printing.
